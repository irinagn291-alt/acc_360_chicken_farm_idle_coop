# Chicken Farm Idle: Coop

## What it is

“Chicken Farm Idle: Coop” is a single-player idle farming game for people who enjoy short, low-pressure check-ins. Players feed hens, collect eggs, sell them or fill neighbor orders, and spend farm coins on hens and upgrades. The farm produces eggs between visits while the trough has grain and the nest has room.

## Launch and onboarding

1. A normal cold launch opens “Yard” directly, with the navigation title “Chicken Farm Idle: Coop”. There is no welcome screen, tutorial, setup button, or permission prompt.
2. The farm scene shows “A little care.” and “A happy flock.” on separate lines, beside “YOUR FARM”. On a fresh farm, the header starts at “0 care days” and 80 coins. “Hens” shows 3/4, “In the nest” starts at 0/24, and “Eggs in barn” starts at 0.
3. “Nest check” shows an egg countdown. “Collect 0 eggs” is disabled until the first egg is ready, about 40 seconds into a fresh farm. “Feeding time” starts with a full trough, so “Fill the trough” becomes available as the hens use grain. “Today's care” initially says “Collect eggs or fill the trough to keep your care streak going.”
4. The other tabs, “Barn”, “Market”, and “Grow”, are available immediately. On later cold launches, the saved farm opens on “Yard” and updates for the elapsed time.

## Features

- “Nest check” with timed egg production, limited nest capacity, and collection into the barn.
- “Feeding time” with grain consumption, a feed countdown, and trough refills from the “Grain reserve”.
- “Today's care”, a consecutive-day care streak, and a lifetime egg collection count.
- “A walk in the meadow” with free grain every 30 minutes.
- “Farm stall” and “Stock up” for selling eggs and buying grain with farm coins.
- “Neighbor orders” with three repeat customers and changing requests.
- “The weekly fair” with three goals, a Monday reset, and a 120-coin reward.
- “Welcome a hen”, “Coop expansion”, “Soft nesting beds”, and “Bigger grain store”.
- “Gentle reminders” for a full nest, an empty trough, and a new weekly fair.
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, automatic farm resume, and “Start a new farm” with confirmation.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “Collect 0 eggs” and “Sell 0 eggs” are disabled on a fresh farm. Wait for an egg, collect it on “Yard”, then sell it on “Market”. Collection takes all ready eggs. Selling takes all collected eggs.
- “Production is paused until you add grain.” means the trough is empty. Use “Fill the trough” on “Yard” or “Barn”. A full trough also disables “Fill the trough” until the flock consumes some grain. If the barn is empty, use “Gather 10 grain” or “Buy grain” first. “The nest is full. Time to gather!” means the nest has stopped accumulating eggs while the hens continue to eat. Collect the eggs to free space. Eggs already in the nest can still be collected after the trough runs dry.
- “Gather 10 grain” is disabled while “Ready in” is counting down or the grain reserve is full. Wait for the timer, or make space by moving grain into the trough. If fewer than 10 units fit, gathering adds only what fits and starts the cooldown. “Buy grain” needs 12 coins and room for all 20 grain. Earn coins from eggs or the fair, use stored grain, or upgrade “Bigger grain store”.
- Each “Deliver eggs” button stays disabled until “Eggs in barn” covers that card’s “Needs {number} eggs”. Delivering refreshes the same card. The list stays at three cards, and each customer’s requirement rises by two per delivery through four values before cycling back to its starting amount.
- “Collect 120 coins” stays disabled until all three fair goals are met. “Fair completed” stays disabled after the reward is claimed. On Monday in the device’s time zone, the fair counters and claim status reset, and any unclaimed reward from the previous week expires. Neighbor orders keep their current requests.
- “Add hen · {number} coins” needs enough coins and an open slot. When “Expand the coop to welcome more hens.” appears, buy a “Coop expansion” if another level is available. “Improve · {number} coins” requires enough coins and, for the two locked upgrades, the first “Coop expansion”. “Fully grown” is the permanent final state of a completed upgrade.
- “{number} care days” starts at zero. Collecting eggs, filling the trough, or gathering grain counts once per local calendar day. Care on the next consecutive day adds one. After a missed day, the streak returns to zero and the next care action starts it at one.
- “Disabled in device Settings” means iOS permission must be changed in the device’s notification settings. Farming remains available. Widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS, so the widget can lag behind the open farm.
- “Progress uses this device's clock. Moving the clock back pauses progress until the saved time is reached.” Wait until the clock reaches the last recorded farm time for timed production to resume.
- A recovered farm shows “Your farm was recovered from its local backup.” If neither saved copy can be read, the app starts a fresh farm and shows “The saved file could not be read. A fresh farm is open; the original file is kept on this device.” If saving fails, it shows “Saving is unavailable. Keep the app open and free some device storage.”

## Starter content and resume

A new farm starts with 3 hens, 80 farm coins, 48 stored grain, 12 grain in the trough, no eggs, no care streak, and no upgrades. Starting capacity is 4 hens, 24 eggs in the nest, 80 stored grain, and 12 grain in the trough. The three neighbor orders and current weekly fair are available immediately.

Farm progress saves automatically and resumes when the app reopens. Time away can produce eggs up to nest capacity, limited by available trough grain. At the starting flock size, a full trough lasts 40 minutes. A normal relaunch opens “Yard” with the existing farm.

## Permissions

Optional iOS notification permission for alerts and sounds is requested when the player taps “Enable reminders” in “Farm settings”. There is no app-supplied usage-description string. The in-app explanation is “Choose reminders for a full nest, an empty trough, and the start of the weekly fair. Your farm works the same if you decline.”

The notification title is “Chicken Farm Idle: Coop”. The possible messages are “Your nest is full. Collect the eggs when you visit.”, “The trough is empty. Bring grain to keep eggs coming.”, and “A new weekly fair is open in your farm.”

## Absent

Login and accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are all absent.

## Data and support

The farm and reminder preferences stay on the device. Installed farm widgets use the local farm progress. The game works offline, and removing the app removes its farm. There is no on-screen “Contact” or “Support” control.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

- iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS or iPadOS 17.6.
- iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait. On wider layouts, the farm illustration is larger and “Nest check” and “Feeding time” can appear side by side.
- The native UI is localized in English, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese, with English as the fallback. The display name “Chicken Farm Idle: Coop” is the same in all four. There is no region selector or region-specific gameplay. The coin count follows device number formatting, and care days and the Monday fair reset use the device’s local time.
