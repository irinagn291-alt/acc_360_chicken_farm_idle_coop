# Farm Idle: Coop privacy

Farm Idle: Coop does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, purchases, or network APIs. Farm progress and reminder preferences are stored on the device. A copy of farm progress is shared with the app's own widgets through its private App Group. Notifications are scheduled locally only after permission is requested from the reminder screen. No notification service or server receives farm data.

Use Farm settings → Start a new farm to replace local progress. Removing the application removes its local farm. Device backup behavior is controlled by Apple and your device settings. The game does not use camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, calendars, or health data.

The same policy is available in localized form inside Farm settings. No public privacy-policy URL or support address has been supplied; these must be provided by the publisher before App Store submission.
