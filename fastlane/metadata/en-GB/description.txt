Care for a flock of hens in a light, illustrated farmyard. Keep the trough filled, collect eggs from the nest and take them to market. Sell your eggs for farm coins or use them to fulfil orders from neighbours.

Gather free grain in the meadow or buy more at the market. Spend your farm coins on hens and coop expansions. Expand the coop to unlock improvements to the nesting beds and grain store, so your hens can lay faster and you can hold more feed.

Collect eggs, refill the trough or gather grain to build a daily care streak. Meet all three weekly fair goals to claim a coin reward. Your hens can keep laying while you’re away as long as there’s grain in the trough and room in the nest.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show farm information, and optional reminders let you know when the nest is full, the trough is empty or a new weekly fair begins.
