Care for a flock of hens in a bright, illustrated farmyard. Fill the trough with grain, collect eggs from the nest, and bring them to market. Sell eggs for farm coins or use them to fill your neighbors’ orders.

Gather free grain in the meadow or buy more at the market. Spend your farm coins on new hens and coop expansions. Expand the coop to unlock upgrades for the nesting beds and grain store, so your hens can lay faster and you can store more feed.

Collect eggs, refill the trough, or gather grain to build a daily care streak. Complete all three weekly fair goals to claim a coin reward. While you’re away, your hens can keep laying as long as the trough has grain and the nest has room.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad. Check your farm with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and turn on optional reminders for a full nest, an empty trough, or the start of a new weekly fair.
