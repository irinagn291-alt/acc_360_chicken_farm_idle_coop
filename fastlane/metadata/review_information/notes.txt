The app opens directly in the Yard without onboarding or sign-in. A fresh farm has 3 hens, 80 coins, 48 grain in reserve, 12 grain in the trough and no collected eggs. There is no production demo mode.

In the Yard, refill the trough and leave grain available. Each starter hen lays one egg every 120 seconds at the initial rate while the trough has grain and the nest has space. The app calculates elapsed progress from the device clock, including time away. Moving the clock backwards pauses progress until the saved time is reached. Collect whole eggs into the Barn, then open Market to sell them or deliver an order once you have enough eggs. Market also offers grain and a weekly fair. In Barn, gather free meadow grain. In Grow, buy hens within capacity and expand the coop to unlock nesting bed and grain store upgrades. Settings includes a farm reset with confirmation.

Notification permission is requested only when reminders are enabled in Settings. The reminders are local and cover a full nest, an empty trough and a new weekly fair. The project declares a push entitlement and remote notification background mode. The app does not register with APNs or implement remote delivery. It does not use camera, microphone, location, photo, biometric or accessory permissions. There are no accounts, purchases, ads, analytics or network integrations.

The farm and its backup are saved locally. An App Group shares a farm snapshot with WidgetKit for Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. The system controls refresh timing, and tapping a widget opens the Yard. The meadowquill URL scheme opens Barn or Market by host and defaults to Yard. The app supports iPhone and iPad on iOS 17.6 or later.
